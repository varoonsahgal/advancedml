The one idea behind everything

Define "good", measure its gradient, take a step. Repeat.

The through-line

One update rule, five disguises

$$\theta \;\leftarrow\; \theta \;-\; \eta\,\nabla_\theta\, \mathcal{L}(\theta)$$
Monθ is a function: each boosting tree is one step.
Tueθ is millions of weights; ∇ comes from autograd.
Wed𝓛 is next-token cross-entropy.
Thu𝓛 regresses a velocity field.
Fri𝓛 is a reward you can verify.
Come back to this slide every morning. Ask: what are θ, 𝓛 and ∇ today?
🍯 Key takeaway

Every model this week is a loss, a gradient and a step. Only the disguise changes.

When something new looks scary, ask: what is being optimized, what is the gradient of, and what does one step change?
