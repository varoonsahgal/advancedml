Day 4 · generative models

Noise → data with flow matching

Each particle starts as Gaussian noise and follows the exact flow-matching velocity field toward a target shape. Type your name into "text". Then drop to 1–2 steps to see why few-step generation is the hard frontier.

Day 2 · optimization

The optimizer arena

Same start, same learning rate, different update rules. Click the landscape to move the start point. Can you find a setting where momentum overshoots and Adam doesn't? Where Lion's sign updates rattle around?

Day 1 · generalization

Bias–variance, live

The faint honey curves are fits to other random training sets drawn from the same distribution; their spread is the variance. Find the degree that minimizes test MSE for 10 points, then for 100.

Day 3 · inference

Decoding lab

Temperature, top-k, top-p and min-p, all composable. Crank the temperature to 2.5 and the bees start making taxes. Then add min-p = 0.1 and watch it clean up the tail.
